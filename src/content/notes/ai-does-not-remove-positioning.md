AI makes it easier to create acceptable work. That is exactly why acceptable work is becoming less valuable.

The differentiator is not access to generation. It is a clear point of view: what you choose to make, who it is for and what you deliberately refuse to become.

## Speed exposes sameness

When every competitor can publish quickly, generic language spreads even faster.

The answer is not to avoid AI. The answer is to give it better source material: real customer conversations, decisions, data, scars and opinions earned through work.

## Positioning is the filter

A strong position helps you decide what not to publish, what not to build and which customers are not a fit.

AI can multiply your output. Positioning decides whether that output compounds into a recognizable brand.

## What it is good at

Notion is a strong home for editorial calendars, client knowledge, reusable templates and lightweight internal operating systems.

Its value appears when information is easy to find, consistently structured and connected to actual work.

## Where it earns its place

Use it for:

- a content pipeline from idea to published asset;
- project dashboards and decision logs;
- a searchable prompt and workflow library;
- client or team knowledge that evolves over time.

## The H² rule

Start with a small database and one repeatable process.

Build complexity only after people use the simple version consistently.

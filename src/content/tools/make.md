## The short version

Make is the automation layer to choose when the process is more important than the trigger. It lets you map a workflow visually: an enquiry arrives, information is checked, AI turns it into a draft, a person approves it and the final record reaches the right system.

That visibility is the point. It makes Make a good fit for small businesses that have outgrown copying information between email, forms, spreadsheets, a CRM and project tools.

## Where it earns its place

Use Make when you need to:

- connect several steps instead of a single app-to-app handoff;
- route a lead, client brief or support request based on its contents;
- create an AI-assisted draft that stays reviewable before it reaches a customer;
- keep a record of what happened when a workflow fails;
- gradually improve a process without rebuilding it from scratch.

## A practical starting setup

Begin with one workflow that has a clear owner. For example: new enquiry → structured brief → missing-information questions → human review → CRM or project board.

1. Write the manual version of the process first.
2. Connect only the two systems that create the most copying or delay.
3. Let AI classify or draft; do not let it make a commercial promise on its own.
4. Add an error notification and a simple weekly review of failed runs.

## The H² rule

Automate the handoff, not the judgment.

Make is most useful when it removes repetitive movement between tools while a person still owns the decision that affects a customer, payment or public claim.
